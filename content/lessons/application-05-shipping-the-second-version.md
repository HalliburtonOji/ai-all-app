## Why this matters

You shipped v1 in an afternoon with AI. Felt like magic. Six months later you can spot the AI fingerprints on it from a mile away — the safe phrasing, the hedged advice, the smoothness where there should be edges.

V2 is where you turn it into work that holds up.

## What v1 usually has

- Generic structure (because the model picked the safest one)
- One or two phrases the model uses too often
- Hedges where the original draft probably had a stronger opinion
- Smoothing where there should be a rough edge that's *yours*

## What v2 needs

1. **Cuts.** The first thing you'll notice on re-read is filler. Cut it.
2. **Sharpening.** Where v1 said "this is helpful," v2 says exactly *what's* helpful and *for whom*. Specifics.
3. **Voice restoration.** Re-read v1 out loud. Anywhere it sounds like a smart-but-anonymous person, replace it with how *you* would actually say it.
4. **One uncomfortable line.** v1 is probably too tidy. Add the one line that wouldn't have made it through "professional-sounding" filter. That's where your actual voice lives.

## The v2 routine

Schedule it. Don't try to do v2 in the same session as v1; you're too close. The minimum gap is overnight; better is 3-7 days.

When you sit down for v2:

- Print or paste somewhere new (a different app helps).
- Read it like a stranger.
- Make every cut you flinch about. The piece is almost always too long.
- Add the one specific anecdote, number, or named thing the v1 didn't have.

## Where AI is useful in v2 (specifically)

- **As a critic.** Paste v1, ask "where would a sharp first reader stop reading?" Sometimes useful, sometimes vague.
- **For the edit pass mechanics.** Cut padding, flag clichés, surface where you generalised. AI is decent at the routine pass.
- **Never for v2 in your voice.** That's the whole point.

## Where it isn't

- **Choosing what to cut.** Models tend to preserve everything; you need to be willing to cut a section the model might say is "valuable."
- **Adding the uncomfortable line.** Models smooth toward safe-average. You can't outsource the bit that's actually you.

## Try it

Pick something you shipped 1-3 weeks ago that involved AI. Apply the v2 routine. Notice which changes feel like real upgrades vs cosmetic. The real upgrades are usually the cuts and the voice restoration. Save those moves; they're the ones that compound.
